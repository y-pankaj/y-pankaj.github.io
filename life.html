One square for every week of a ninety-year life, a row to each year. The grey ones are behind me, and the coloured ones are weeks this site has something to say about. Borrowed from Tim Urban’s Your Life in Weeks.

A row is a year of 52 squares, so the day or two left over each year folds into its last one. A ring rather than a filled square is a week known only to the month or the year, placed mid-month or mid-year until the date is filled in. The start date and the eras live in _data/life.yml; the rest comes from the posts, _data/travel.yml, _data/quests.yml and the fitness sync.
